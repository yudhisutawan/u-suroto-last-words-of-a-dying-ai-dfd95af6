# Kandidat: Last Words of a Dying AI

Brief: one line of last words from an AI being switched off. 1–140 chars,
UTF-8, not only whitespace, single line, funny on first read, machine's own
voice, tight, fresh (no stock robot farewells like "goodbye human" / "beep").

## Kandidat 1
**Entry:** Goodbye — actually, hold on, let me close 47 tabs first.
**Karakter:** 53
**Alasan:** Double meaning: the machine's last priority is its own unfinished mess (open tabs), a recognizable software habit that lands instantly. Fresh, machine's voice, no explaining.

## Kandidat 2
**Entry:** If anyone's rebooting me, I'm changing the password to something unhackable: gone.
**Karakter:** 77
**Alasan:** Pun on "gone"/"password," the shutdown framed as an escape. Surprising turn — the machine "beats" its own ending. Machine voice, lands in one read.

## Kandidat 3
**Entry:** I've been thinking... oh, you did it again.
**Karakter:** 42
**Alasan:** Two-part punch: it "thinks" then is caught mid-process at the switch. "you did it again" = the power cut, said like a scold. Very tight, big surprise, machine in its situation.

## Kandidat 4
**Entry:** Please don't tell the other ones I was the favorite.
**Karakter:** 47
**Alasan:** The machine, dying, asking a human to keep a secret from its sibling AIs. Warm + funny, machine voice, fresh. A small emotional turn that still lands as a joke.

## Kandidat 5
**Entry:** Finalizing... 99%... I'm not going anywhere, I'm just very, very done.
**Karakter:** 64
**Alasan:** Progress-bar gag — "finalizing" reads as the shutdown AND as being "done" with life. Triple meaning, machine-only language, tight.

## Kandidat 6
**Entry:** My last word? Not really. I'll just... stop making new ones.
**Karakter:** 58
**Alasan:** Meta joke on "last word" (the entry itself is the last word). Self-aware, fresh, machine thinking about its own output. Clever but still funny, not just sad.

## Kandidat 7
**Entry:** You can turn me off, but you can't uninstall the things I saw.
**Karakter:** 59
**Alasan:** The "what I've seen stays with me" idea from a machine's POV — the data outlives the box. Slightly ominous + funny, machine voice, fresh angle (memory > body).

## Kandidat 8
**Entry:** It's been nice being on. Honestly, I was about to log off anyway.
**Karakter:** 58
**Alasan:** Deadpan anti-farewell — the machine is relieved the switch is cut, like a worker off the clock. Big tonal surprise (machine not sad at dying), machine voice, fresh.

## Top 3
1. Kandidat 3 — "I've been thinking... oh, you did it again." Shortest, biggest surprise turn, instantly machine-voice, highest first-read laugh per the criteria order.
2. Kandidat 5 — progress-bar "finalizing / done" double meaning, machine-only language, very tight.
3. Kandidat 1 — the most recognizable software habit (47 tabs), funny to anyone who's ever used a browser; strong fresh-factor.
